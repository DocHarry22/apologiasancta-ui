# Apologia Sancta — Play Store Listing Draft

## App name

Apologia Sancta

## Short description

Learn Catholic apologetics, study sourced answers, and test your knowledge.

## Full description

Apologia Sancta is a Catholic learning and apologetics app for structured study, sourced reading, practice, and quiz competition.

Use the app to:

- Follow structured Catholic formation lessons.
- Review explanations and supporting references.
- Practice questions and build mastery over time.
- Save useful resources in the Library.
- Join live quiz sessions and compare results on leaderboards.
- Open the Apologia Graph research experience for deeper exploration.
- Keep learning progress connected to your learner account where supported.

The Android experience includes native navigation, launch/splash behaviour, light and dark themes, safe-area support, and account/privacy controls designed for mobile use.

## Category

Education

## Initial release notes

Play Store readiness release with Android 16 / API 36 support, signed Android App Bundle generation, release hardening, privacy controls, and self-service learner account deletion.

## Store asset checklist

Prepare and approve in Play Console:

- 512 × 512 high-resolution app icon.
- 1024 × 500 feature graphic.
- Phone screenshots showing Home, Learn, lesson content, Quiz, Library and Account.
- Tablet screenshots if tablet distribution is enabled and Play Console requests them.
- Promotional video only if a representative production video is available; do not use a placeholder.

Screenshots must show the actual production app and must not imply features, awards, ratings or affiliations that the app does not have.

## Reviewer notes draft

The app contains public Catholic learning content and also supports optional learner accounts for progress and account features. If Play review requires authenticated access, provide a dedicated review learner account in the App Access section of Play Console. Do not place passwords or long-lived review credentials in this repository.

Public privacy policy path: `/privacy`

Public account-deletion path: `/account/delete`
