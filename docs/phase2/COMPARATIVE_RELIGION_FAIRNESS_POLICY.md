# Comparative-religion fairness policy

## Scope before claim

Never use a family label where the position varies within that family. A claim records a named tradition, denomination, school, confession, jurisdiction or named argument. Examples of acceptable scopes include Eastern Orthodox, Lutheran, Reformed, Anglican, Baptist, Methodist, Pentecostal, non-denominational evangelical, Sunni, Shi’a, Ahmadi, Jehovah’s Witness, Latter-day Saint, and a named atheist philosophical school or argument.

The generic phrases “Protestants believe” and “Muslims believe” are blocking validation errors unless the content explicitly discusses the linguistic category itself and then documents the diversity.

## Evidence order

Prefer, in order:

1. an official confession, catechism, council, governing document or recognised institutional statement;
2. a representative primary author recognised within the tradition;
3. a credible specialist scholar accurately describing the scoped position;
4. a carefully qualified synthesis when primary documents do not answer the exact question.

A critic of a tradition is not the primary source for what that tradition teaches. Internal diversity, historical change and jurisdictional differences must be stated where material.

## Steelman requirement

Objections at Objections or Synthesis level, and all Expert comparative questions, require a `steelman` field. It must state the position in a form an informed defender could recognise, include the strongest relevant premise, and avoid importing a Catholic assumption into the opponent’s definition.

The Catholic response may identify contradiction, insufficiency or error, but first distinguishes the position actually held from weaker popular variants.

## Review checklist

- Is the tradition named precisely?
- Is the cited document authoritative or genuinely representative for that scope?
- Does the text distinguish historical and present forms?
- Would an informed defender recognise the summary?
- Are quotations and translations accurate and licensed?
- Does the distractor explanation correct an error without ridicule?
- Has a comparative reviewer flagged any unresolved representation dispute?

Material disagreement by a qualified reviewer triggers `disputed_or_unresolved` and blocks publication pending adjudication.
