# Review workflow and checklists

Required sequence:

`draft → author review → doctrinal review → assessment review → source/licence review → approval → publication → analytics review`

All review records are version-specific. Editing reviewed content creates a new version and invalidates prior-version approval for publication.

## Roles and separation of duties

| Stage | Who may act | Core restriction |
|---|---|---|
| Draft/author review | Author/contributor for own item; editor/admin | Author review is not final approval |
| Doctrinal review | Reviewer with doctrinal specialism | Must be qualified; cannot rely on machine classification |
| Assessment review | Reviewer with assessment specialism | Required for every question; independent for Trick/Expert |
| Source/licence review | Reviewer with source/licence specialism | Permission evidence, quotation limits and attribution |
| Approval | Editor/admin/super-admin with approval authority | High-risk creator cannot be the sole final approver |
| Publication | Publisher permission after current-version approval | Database blocks unresolved errors |
| Analytics review | Analytics reviewer after publication | May recommend revision/quarantine, never relock |

A platform role alone does not assert theological qualification. Reviewer specialisms are explicit assignments with approver, evidence note and optional expiry.

## Author checklist

- Central question and objectives are precise.
- All fifteen lesson components exist or reviewed non-applicability is recorded.
- Every claim has a type and attribution mode.
- Questions link to taught objectives.
- Comparative scopes are named and steelmanned where required.
- Citations and rights data are complete.
- No generated text is treated as a source.

## Doctrinal checklist

- Exact propositions, not paragraphs, are classified.
- Source genre, authority, scope and intent support the classification.
- Dogma/definitive/authoritative/disciplinary/prudential categories are not conflated.
- Fathers, Doctors and private theologians are properly identified.
- Interpretations and inferences are labelled.
- Uncertainty is marked for adjudication rather than guessed.

## Assessment checklist

- Stem is self-contained and tests the objective.
- Exactly one best answer and four enabled options.
- Each distractor is plausible, explained and misconception-coded.
- No prohibited option pattern or unintended clue.
- Level and mode match the cognitive task.
- Expert/Trick requirements and approved category are met.
- Question is mastery, not disconnected trivia.

## Source/licence checklist

- Locator resolves to the intended edition.
- Authority category is correct.
- Quotation/paraphrase status and word count are accurate.
- Translation, copyright, permission, licence and attribution are recorded.
- Product channel, monetisation, offline and AI/ML uses are permitted.
- Review/expiry date and prohibited-use flags are current.

## Approval/publication checklist

- Required current-version reviews are approved.
- High-risk separation of duties is satisfied.
- No blocking validation issue remains.
- Scheduled/public visibility is intentional.
- Engine and Graph relationships validate.
- Rollback/quarantine path exists.

## Analytics checklist

- Difficulty and distractor statistics suggest a valid item.
- No sign of ambiguity, answer leakage or tradition-specific unfairness.
- Misconceptions map to corrective material.
- Retention recognition is separate from unlock status.
- Recommended edits create a new version; they do not rewrite attempt history.
