# Unresolved policy decisions requiring human review

This file is intentionally non-empty. Phase 2 creates enforceable boundaries but does not pretend that software can supply ecclesial authority, legal permission or reviewer qualification.

1. **Qualified doctrinal reviewer criteria.** Define required formation, ecclesial accountability, conflict-of-interest rules and who may grant/expire the `doctrinal` specialism.
2. **Final doctrinal-note adjudication.** A competent theologian must approve any concrete use of `dogma`, `definitively_held` or `disputed_or_unresolved`; the classification list is a workflow taxonomy, not an automated theological judgment.
3. **Local ecclesiastical oversight.** Determine whether the project will seek a diocesan censor/imprimatur or other formal oversight for any publication category, and in which jurisdiction.
4. **Bible translation licences.** No modern translation is approved for in-app quotation by default. Obtain written product-specific terms for web, mobile, offline, audio and AI/ML use.
5. **Vatican and other official-text reuse.** Confirm permissible quotation/republication terms with the relevant rightsholder; official availability does not equal a blanket licence.
6. **Initial approved-source registry.** Domain quality, canonical mirrors, redirects and archival copies require a human-maintained list. Domain approval must remain distinct from reuse permission.
7. **Quotation limits by source.** Legal/licensing review must set each non-zero limit and attribution template. The machine fallback remains zero.
8. **Accessibility exception to four options.** Define who may approve it, supported interface needs, and whether such an item may enter official/live pools.
9. **Threshold exceptions.** Determine whether any group may use less than 100% official mastery and the evidence/approval required. Default remains 100%.
10. **Retake equivalence policy.** Curriculum owners must define equivalence keys and minimum alternative-pool size per objective.
11. **High-risk content scope.** Phase 2 treats lessons, lesson sections, doctrinal claims, questions and sources as high risk. A qualified governance board should ratify or expand this list.
12. **Comparative reviewer roster.** Identify qualified reviewers for specific traditions rather than granting one universal comparative credential.
13. **Analytics thresholds.** Set sample-size, discrimination, ambiguity and quarantine thresholds only after real, ethically collected data exists.
14. **Legacy content disposition.** Existing illustrative Graph records and any pre-Phase-2 questions remain “editorial review required”; they are not silently grandfathered as approved.
15. **South African data and education context.** Confirm consumer, child-safety, privacy, accessibility and educational claims with qualified local counsel and educators before monetised or school deployment.
