## Architecture

The compulsory route begins with grp.catholic-foundations.foundations and ends at grp.moral-prayer-foundations.synthesis. Specialist programmes then run in parallel. Bible-book tracks unlock independently after Scripture-and-canon distinctions and never depend on another Bible book.

Totals: 8 programmes, 118 subjects (including 73 Bible books), 590 groups, and 815 lesson outlines.

Estimated study: 43.1 hours

Unlock: entry route
