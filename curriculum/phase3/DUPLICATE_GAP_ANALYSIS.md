## Open gaps

### gap.graph-taxonomy (high)

The current graph taxonomy has no dedicated categories for sacraments generally, moral theology, prayer, liturgy, social teaching, bioethics, Church Fathers, councils, or most biblical books.

Disposition: Keep empty or nearest-category links in draft; require graph-taxonomy approval before lesson production.

### gap.scripture-licensing (critical)

No repository policy identifies approved Bible translations, quotation limits, territories, or APK/offline redistribution terms.

Disposition: Phase 4 must store references and original paraphrases until a written licensing policy is approved.

### gap.lesson-block-schema (critical)

The existing editorial workflow validates quiz Question records, not the complete structured lesson-block model assumed by Phase 4.

Disposition: Approve and implement the lesson block schema and revision workflow before full lesson bodies are imported.

### gap.comparative-reviewers (critical)

Named qualified reviewers for Orthodox, Islamic, Jewish, world-religion, NRM, science, bioethics, and abuse-sensitive material are not yet assigned.

Disposition: Assign reviewers and escalation rules before those lesson batches move beyond draft.

### gap.source-languages (medium)

The blueprint does not presume staff competence in Greek, Hebrew, Latin, Syriac, Arabic, or other source languages.

Disposition: Record translation editions and require specialist review when an argument turns on original-language claims.
